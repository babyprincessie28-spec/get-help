While you wait

Broken down on the road
  • Turn on hazard lights and pull as far off the road as you safely can.
  • Everyone out of the car on the side away from traffic, if it is safe to leave it.
  • Stay visible and away from the vehicle on busy highways.
  • Share your location, then call a tow truck or roadside cover.
Accident with injuries
  • Call the ambulance number first and say exactly where you are.
  • Do not move an injured person unless there is fire or immediate danger.
  • Press firmly on heavy bleeding with a clean cloth.
  • Stay on the line until the operator tells you to hang up.
Car fire or smoke
  • Stop, switch off the engine and get everyone out.
  • Move well away from the car and call the fire number.
  • Do not open the bonnet; fresh air feeds the fire.
Feeling unsafe or followed
  • Drive or walk to a busy, well-lit place such as a petrol station or police station.
  • Keep doors locked and call the police number.
  • Share your live location with someone you trust.

This page opens Google Maps searches and phone calls. It cannot guarantee a business is open, nearby or reputable, so check before you pay. Emergency numbers are listed for convenience; confirm the correct ones for where you are.
